# 🚀 VibeWave v2.0.7 — Streak Multiplier Engine, MiniPlayer Live XP HUD & 1-Tap AI Mood Carousel

Welcome to **VibeWave v2.0.7**! This major release supercharges gamification, visual motion, and the AI Companion experience to make listening to music deeply rewarding, modern, and engaging.

---

### 🎨 1. Real-Time Live XP & Micro-Equalizer HUD on MiniPlayer
- **Live Frequency Waveform Visualizer**: The MiniPlayer now features an active, animated 3-bar micro-equalizer that rhythmically oscillates while music is playing.
- **Real-Time XP Rate Badge**: A sleek, frosted cyan-purple glowing HUD badge (`⚡ +5 XP/m` up to `⚡ +10 XP/m`) dynamically displays your current XP accumulation rate in real time, factoring in your active streak multiplier!

---

### ⚡ 2. Streak Multipliers Engine & Daily Mystery Vibe Box
- **Streak Multiplier System**: Maintaining a daily streak now supercharges your Vibe XP gain across all listening minutes, track plays, and mystery rewards:
  - **3–6 Days**: **1.2x XP Multiplier** (`🔥 3 • 1.2x`)
  - **7–13 Days**: **1.5x Golden Flame Multiplier** (`🔥 7 • 1.5x`)
  - **14+ Days**: **2.0x Godmode Double XP** (`🔥 14 • 2.0x`)
- **Dynamic Top-Bar Flame**: The streak pill in the top bar dynamically adapts its gradient (orange, gold, or cosmic purple) and reflects your active multiplier tag.
- **🎁 Daily Mystery Vibe Box**:
  - Visit the **Engagement Center** once every 24 hours to open your daily gift box!
  - Awards between **+25 to +100 bonus Vibe XP**, boosted by your active streak multiplier.
  - Accompanied by the celebratory unlock modal with XP reward breakdown.
- **Leaderboard Integration**: Leaderboard rows and user profile banners now proudly display the active multiplier alongside playback minutes and tracks.

  - 🎲 **Surprise Me** — Trending viral hits & undiscovered gems

- **Expanded AI Knowledge**: VibeWave AI now answers detailed inquiries about Streak Multipliers, Mystery Vibe Boxes, and XP mechanics.
