# 🚀 VibeWave v2.0.6 — Whole-App VibeWave AI Companion & Redesigned Gemini Bar

Welcome to **VibeWave v2.0.6**! This release completely overhauls the AI experience in VibeWave. We redesigned the bulky AI card on the Home screen into a sleek, uncluttered **Gemini-Style AI Bar & Toggle**, and introduced the **Full-App VibeWave AI Companion** — a smart, interactive AI you can chat with from anywhere in the app to discover music, get tailored song recommendations with 1-tap playback, and answer any app-related questions.

---

### ✨ Redesigned Gemini AI Bar & Header Toggle
- **Uncluttered & Modern**: The old bulky AI card under "Listen Now" has been redesigned into an ultra-sleek, 48dp-tall frosted Gemini bar with a radiant holographic outline.
- **Header "Ask AI ✨" Pill**: Added a compact Gemini spark toggle right beside the "Listen Now" title.
- **1-Tap Expansion**: Tapping either the header pill or the Gemini bar slides up the full descriptive **VibeWave AI Companion Studio & Chat**.

---

### 🤖 Whole-App Interactive VibeWave AI Companion
- **Real Conversational Chat**: A dedicated AI assistant powered by on-device neural acoustic intelligence without external paid API keys.
- **App Knowledge Expert**: Instantly answers questions about:
  - *Daily Streaks & Flame badges*
  - *Vibe XP & Level progression (Levels 1–25)*
  - *Real-time Listen Together with Host DJ control*
  - *Engagement Center, Daily Quests & Leaderboards*
  - *10-Band Studio Equalizer & Bass Punch calibration*
  - *Offline downloads & real-time synced lyrics*
- **Smart Music Matchmaker with 1-Tap Playback**:
  - Ask for any mood, genre, artist, or activity (e.g., *"Recommend late night romantic Bollywood songs"*, *"Give me heavy bass gym phonk"*).
  - The AI curates real tracks from YouTube Music and embeds interactive song cards directly in the chat with artwork, artist, and **"▶ Play"** / **"Play All"** buttons!
- **Quick-Prompt Suggestions Carousel**: Fast 1-tap prompts to immediately start chatting and discovering music.

---

### 🌐 Universal Top-Bar AI Spark Button
- Added a sparkling Gemini icon (`✨`) to the frosted top bar across all screens, allowing you to summon your AI Companion anytime, whether you are browsing your Library, Explore, or Home feed.
